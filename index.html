The recipe

  1. True black canvas, one mint accent, gray for everything secondary.
  2. Logo as geometry: a simple solid, matte dark material, white specular edges defining the form.
  3. Package as squircle icons with grain, vignette, and contact shadow.
  4. Animate with a slow tumble — let the mark be abstract for part of the loop.
  5. UI: huge thin numerals, small-caps gray labels, hairline dashed gridlines, zero decorative color.
