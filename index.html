The recipe

  1. One silhouette. A bespoke letterform, not a font — flat cuts, bladed terminals, confident weight.
  2. Metal, not color. Brushed steel on true black; the accent green is for product UI, never the mark.
  3. Triple carried inside. Three S’s expressed as cuts, depth, or rotation within one shape — never spelled out.
  4. Package as hardware. Squircle, grain, vignette, contact shadow — the mark should look machined, then photographed.
  5. Then motion. The chosen mark gets the slow tumble with specular sweep.
